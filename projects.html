Our work

Projects that turn local questions into useful evidence.

We work with communities, schools and researchers to observe local environments over time, strengthen the quality of observations and make findings useful to the people involved.

Propose a project

How our projects work

Each project follows a simple cycle: identify a question, collect observations, check their quality, connect them with wider knowledge and share the results with participants.

Ask

Start with a practical environmental question that a community, school or partner needs answered.

Observe

Use clear methods and repeat observations over time so changes and patterns can be understood.

Validate

Review observations, clarify methods and involve scientific partners where appropriate.

Connect

Organise findings so they can be compared, interpreted and used alongside other information.

Return

Share accessible findings with the people who collected the observations and with relevant local partners.

Current projects

Water

Community water observatory

Community participants and students make repeated observations of water conditions at selected sites. Scientific partners support training, quality assurance and interpretation.

Focus: water conditions, seasonal change and local observations.

Who can take part: community members, students, schools and environmental organisations.

Schools

School environmental observatories

A school adopts a nearby stream, wetland, green space or rainfall point and observes it across the year. Environmental learning becomes participation in real research.

Focus: practical science learning, local environmental change and student-led observation.

Who can take part: teachers, learners, school leaders and local partners.
